A **shelf** is one public page. It has **sections**, and sections hold **links**.

## Build your first shelf

1. Sign in and open **Shelf** in the sidebar.
2. Create a shelf: title, description, icon, and a [path or domain](/docs/usage/shelves#path-or-domain).
3. Open it and add a section, for example `Coding`.
4. Add links to the section: title, URL, icon, optional color.
5. Drag sections and links into the order you want, then press **Save order**.
6. Pick a [theme](/docs/usage/themes) in the shelf settings.
7. Press **Open** to see the public page.

## Share it

On the shelf page:

- **Copy URL**
- **QR code**, to print or show on a screen

## The rest of the app

- **Dashboard**: number of shelves, sections and links, plus your recent shelves
- **Themes**: your own themes, see [Themes](/docs/usage/themes)
- **Profile**: name, username, password
- **Settings**: admins only, see [Admin](/docs/usage/admin)
