LinkShelf Cloud is available. It is the managed version of LinkShelf: we run, update and back up
everything, so you can create your shelves and share them without maintaining a server.

It runs the same open-source code you can [self-host](/docs/self-hosting/getting-started) at any time.

## Sign up

See the plans and pricing at [account.linkshelf.cloud](https://account.linkshelf.cloud), then create
your account there. You can sign in with your email and password, or with GitHub or Google.

Once you are signed in, continue with the [usage guide](/docs/usage/getting-started) to build and share
your first shelf.

## Verify your email

After you register, you need to verify your email address before you can sign in. Open the
verification link we send you, then return to the sign-in page. If you don't see the email, check your
spam folder.
